Three ways to start

01

AI workflow audit

Who it's for

Teams already paying for AI tools who can't point to the hours they save.

What I do

I look at how your team actually spends its week and find the workflows where AI would save the most time, ranked by effort and payoff.

What you get

A written plan: which workflows, which tools, and what each one should save.

02

Custom AI tool

Who it's for

A team with one repetitive, high-value job that eats hours every week.

What I do

I build an AI tool around that job. One example: a tool that reads a sales rep's accounts and returns recent press, an outreach angle and a priority score for each.

What you get

A working tool your team uses, plus the documentation to keep it running.

Who it's for

Teams told to "use AI" without being shown how it fits their job.

What I do

Hands-on sessions built around your team's own work, not generic prompting tips. People leave with workflows they built themselves.

What you get

A team that can spot AI opportunities and build simple workflows on its own.
