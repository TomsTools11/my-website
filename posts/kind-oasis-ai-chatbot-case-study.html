Kind Oasis is a CBD shop and community space on Milwaukee's East Side. From November 2024 to October 2025 I was their Head of Software & AI. My job was to find the places where custom software and AI could take work off the team, then build it. That covered an internal tool for marketing requests, planning for a wholesale portal, and the project this post is about: an AI shopping assistant on the store's website.

What we built

The assistant runs on VanChat and introduces itself to shoppers as a CBD, Delta 8 and Delta 9 expert. It answers product questions and points people to the products that fit what they're looking for, at any hour, which a small store's staff can't do.

The numbers

These cover the first 60 days, May 27 to July 26, 2025, comparing the first 30 days with the second.

  • Sales through the chatbot grew 83%, from $9,885 in the first 30 days to $18,084 in the second. That's $27,567 over the 60 days.
  • 28.6% of the people who chatted added something to their cart (509 of 1,780). The rate rose from 21.55% in month one to 33.15% in month two.
  • 21.1% of conversations ended in an order: 375 orders from 1,780 conversations.
  • Conversations grew 55.5%, from 710 to 1,104, while site traffic grew 48.1%, so the share of visitors who chatted rose slightly too.
  • Average order value went from $73.33 to $75.50.

What the numbers don't show

  • Everything here comes from VanChat's dashboard. It counts sales VanChat attributes to the chatbot, not the store's total revenue.
  • The comparison is month two against month one with the assistant already running. There's no before-and-after against a period without it.
  • Part of the growth came from more traffic. The best day, $1,980 on June 29, lined up with a promotion.
  • VanChat's report also puts the return on investment above 10,000%. It doesn't show the cost that figure is based on, so I've left it out.
